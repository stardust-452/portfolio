### Research question: What are the genomic difference between Indian Indicine adapted to tropical climate and European Taurine adapted to the temperate regions?
Whole genome sequences from Taurine and Indicine breeds were used to identify the genomic differences responsible for differences in adaption, disease resistance and milk yield.

Additionally, the study involved a haplotype-resolved genome assembly of Sunandini, a crossbreed forming the majority of cattle population in the Indian state of Kerala.

- Conducted variant network analysis using STRING, Cytoscape, and ClueGO to interpret functional relationships between genes.
- Visualized admixture and chromosomal variation using R (karyoplotR, ggplot2) and Python (seaborn, matplotlib).
- Functionally classified and annotated genes using Gene Ontology (GO) and pathway enrichment tools, focusing on milk yield, immunity, and disease resistance traits.
- Performed extensive literature review on cattle admixture and bovine genomic analysis.

**Results:** Identified key variants linked to higher milk yield (taurine) and disease resistance (indicus); functionally characterized genes contributing to these traits and created interaction networks.
