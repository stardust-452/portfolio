### Research question: How does chemotherapy alter transcriptional and chromatin responses associated with DNA damage?
- Analyzed RNA-Seq data for 8 samples for treated & untreated conditions (cardiac cell lines) to find the expressed as part of the DNA Damage Response (DDR)
- ChIP-Seq analysis to find changes in binding of replication associated protein after chemotherapy


**Results:** Identified 506 DEGs, 8 enriched pathways associated with drug response; mapped protein binding site changes.
